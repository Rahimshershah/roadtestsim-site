Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 29, 2026

RoadTest Sim does not collect, store on any server, sell or share your personal information. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

On your phone

Your drive results, progress, language, state and chosen places are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

AI coach

Coaching advice is written on your iPhone by Apple's on-device model (Apple Intelligence) where available. Your driving data is not sent to us or to any AI service.

Maps and addresses

When you search for an address or test center, the search goes to Apple Maps. To build a drivable map, the app requests street data for that area from OpenStreetMap servers (Overpass API), and imagery from Apple Maps. These requests contain the map area, not who you are. They are handled under Apple's privacy policy and the OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy.

Children

The app does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.
