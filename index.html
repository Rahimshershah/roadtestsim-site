RoadTest Sim

A behind-the-wheel driving test simulator for iPhone. Practice the road test on real streets, around your test center, with an AI coach, in six languages, for all 50 states and DC.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Open a support request and we'll get back to you.

Good to know

  • RoadTest Sim is for practicing the behind-the-wheel (road) test only, not the written knowledge test.
  • It is not affiliated with any DMV, MVA or government agency. Rules can change; please check with your state's licensing office.
  • Practice in the app does not replace supervised driving with a licensed adult.
